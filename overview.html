🎶 Audition Toolkit – Overview 🎶

Audition Toolkit by Quantalept is a sleek, modern audio suite for creators, musicians, podcasters, and hobbyists. It blends ease-of-use with powerful editing tools, allowing anyone to turn rough sound into polished, professional audio.

🚀 What It Does

  • Capture and edit audio directly on-device
  • Enhance clarity even in low-quality recordings
  • Apply creative effects (pitch, tempo, EQ, voice manipulation, karaoke extraction)
  • Preview and export clean, ready-to-use audio files

🔧 Current Features

  • Advanced Edit – Precise waveform editing, pitch/tempo, EQ, and fine adjustments
  • Record – Capture high-quality sound directly inside the app
  • Trim – Remove unwanted sections quickly
  • Voice Changer – Transform voices with creative filters
  • Split – Divide tracks or isolate sections
  • Karaoke – Remove vocals for sing-along backing tracks
  • Reverse – Flip audio playback for unique effects
  • Magic Enhancer – AI-powered automatic clarity & tonal balance improvements

⚙️ Extra Functionalities

  • Default save to Music/Audition for convenience
  • Customizable export formats: .mp3, .wav, .aac
  • Preview durations (10s / 20s / 30s)
  • Length filter to skip tiny alert/notification files
  • Recent Files tab for quick access

🎯 Why Choose Audition Toolkit?

  • Clean, intuitive design
  • Beginner-friendly yet powerful enough for professionals
  • All-in-one portable audio studio
  • Smart workflow tweaks to save time and effort
  • Encourages experimentation and creativity
