🗺 Audition Toolkit – Roadmap

📅 December 2025 – Merge Feature

Users will be able to merge more than 5 audio fragments into a single waveform. Both appending (placing clips end-to-end) and overlapping (layering multiple clips together) will be supported for greater flexibility in audio creation.

📅 March 2026 – UI Redesign/Equalizer Feature

A major UI update will refresh the look and feel of the app. This includes improved waveform visualization, redesigned buttons and icons, and the introduction of a Light Mode option alongside the current dark theme.

Additionally, an equalizer feature will be introduced, allowing users to adjust audio frequencies (bass, mid, treble) to enhance their recordings directly within the app.

📅 Mid 2026 – Extended Export & Media Features

- Direct option to set edited audio as a ringtone - Support for adding video alongside audio, enabling overlapping playback and multimedia creation
